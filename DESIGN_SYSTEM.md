# Design System

## Palette
- Jet Black: `#050505` — page background
- Cerulean Blue: `#2A9DDB` — primary actions, orbital light and links
- Neon Rose: `#FF3D81` — restrained highlights and active states
- Electric Lavender: `#A78BFA` — depth and gradient accents
- Ice White: `#F1F5F9` — primary text
- Muted text: `#A7B0BE` — secondary text
- Surface: `#10131B` — elevated panels

## Style
Cinematic, immersive and premium, with generous spacing, clean typography, subtle glass surfaces and carefully controlled glow. Avoid excessive neon, clutter and motion.

## Motion
- Intro sequence: black screen → orbital ring → name → tagline → homepage.
- Use short, purposeful transitions and scroll reveals.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Provide an intro skip control and ensure all content remains accessible if animation is skipped or fails.

## Responsive
- Desktop: broad hero composition with portrait and orbital visual.
- Tablet: simplified 3D composition and compact navigation.
- Mobile: single-column hero, reduced particle count, no heavy interaction required.
